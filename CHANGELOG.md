# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (versionCode 2) – 2026-11-10

- Paddle now follows the finger instantly (no smoothing / lag), fast edge-to-edge thumb movement.
- Bonus life: every 10 hits a glowing heart falls slowly from the top; catch it with the paddle for +1 life.
  A missed heart disappears and costs nothing. Never awarded automatically.
- Lives system replaces one-life: missing the ball uses one life, a new ball drops from the top after a
  short pause; score, level and speed are kept. GAME OVER only when no lives remain.
- Heart life indicator under the score; "BONUS LIFE +1" and "LIFE LOST" messages; new bonus and life-lost sounds.
- How to Play rewritten (basics + bonus life), scrollable, with an animated demo.
- About: email and mobile number are now display-only (tapping no longer opens email or phone apps).

## 1.0.0 (versionCode 1) – 2026-11-09

First release.

- Ball-and-paddle arcade gameplay with gravity and angle-based paddle bounces.
- Difficulty grows with time survived and every 10 hits; speed cap 1.85×; paddle shrinks slightly.
- Strict one-life rule – one miss ends the game.
- Personal best + persistent TOP 3 leaderboard (gold, silver, bronze).
- Game over screen with NEW HIGH SCORE animation, TOP 3 achievement, NEW GAME / MAIN MENU / EXIT GAME.
- Pause with 3-second resume countdown.
- Main menu, How to Play, About (Burhan Nexus logo, clickable email and phone), sound ON/OFF.
- Neon visual style, glow effects, particles, ball trail; responsive for all screen sizes.
- No permissions, fully offline, no ads.
